---
title: Tools for human agency for an uncertain future
---
We are building a new kind of living software, focused on truly supporting people, rather than extracting from them. We are all knowledge workers, and we want to make it easier to make sense of complexity and solve wicked problems together. Seption is the project we are working on to make this possible.

Seption is a project aimed at creating a new paradigm for how we interact with information on our digital devices. We are building a living ecosystem of tools to help us make sense of and navigate the relentless flood of highly interconnected information that defines our modern world. As we face an uncertain future, we hope these tools will increase individual and collective agency.
