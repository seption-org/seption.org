Tools for human agency for an uncertain future.

==We don't have tools to elegantly deal with the complexity of the interconnectedness of the information at hand==

We need tools that give us the agency to make sense of the complex world we live in.

We believe this would be made possible by making it easy to publish, discover, and adapt knowledge.

Our digital information ecosystems are broken:

- Controlled by a few corporations
- It's hard to find what you're looking for
- There's too much information and no good ways to deal with it

- How can we discover and group up with likeminded people?
- How can we organize?
- How can we better share and collaborate on knowledge?

## We are Standing on the Shoulders of Giants

- Tim Berners Lee / The WWW & The Semantic Web
- Ted Nelson / Hypermedia & Transclusion

---

- Human's have not solved the problem of learning how to work with complexity effectively
- We've built and exist in complicated structures that can be simplified
- We are living in times that are both more complicated and more complex than any other time in history and it seems this will only get worse if we don't do something about it
- Complexity is not a problem to solve, it is an organic state that we should strive to flow and grow with instead of fight against
- Tools are extensions of self
- We currently don't have adequate agency over our tools which means we don't have agency over ourselves both individually and collectively
- We need tools that are "cognitive prosthetics" to
  - Help us live in right balanced relationship with complexity
  - Facilitate coordination and collaboration
  - Help us make sense of and simplify complicated things
  - Extend and support our unique cognitive capacities
- Knowledge discovery, production, emergence, and sharing
  - Publishing, discovery, and adaptation/implementation
- We're building a semantic hypergraph as a base layer for the building and reuse of tools and knowledge

In an orwellian way, the tools we use are controlled by companies, which limit our agency, our ability to express ourselves, and our collective social power. As a result, we

- Lack Interoperability (we are trapped by walled app silos)
- Lack Sovereignty (we can't do anything we want with our stuff)
- Lack good design (we are stuck with one-size-fits-none software)
- Lack cognitive support (our abilities are limited, and this can be prevent us from moving forward in the way we wish)
- Lack accessibility to public information and knowledge (we struggle to find what we are looking for)

We need better software infrastructure to make information accessible and empower people to collaborate and innovate, overcoming the limitations of current tools that fail to support interconnected, flexible workflows. Seption aims to provide a new system that models information conceptually, breaking free from restrictive, outdated classification methods, and helping users navigate an increasingly complex world.
